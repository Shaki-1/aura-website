AURA Accessibility Assistant

The Guardian of Accessibility.

AURA is a local-first Windows accessibility assistant for color blindness, eye strain, and low vision. It adapts visual support directly on your desktop without uploading screenshots.

What AURA supports

The Voyager

Color blind support with clearer contrast, stronger color separation, and non-color visual cues.

The Guardian

Eye strain support that softens glare, brightness, and harsh contrast during long sessions.

The Beacon

Low vision support with stronger focus, higher visibility, and reading-area guidance.

How AURA works

1. Choose support

Select Color Blind, Eye Strain, or Low Vision support.

2. Personalize

Choose the profile that best matches your needs and adjust support strength.

3. AURA adapts

AURA analyzes accessible interface information locally and adapts support to your active application.

Private by design

Local First

Processing happens on your computer whenever possible.

No Screenshots

AURA does not capture, save, or upload screenshots.

Your Data Stays Yours

Accessibility support should not require giving away personal information.
